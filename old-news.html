Symbolic Computation Istanbul Meetings {{ site.data.scim.size }} talks

SCIM was a fortnightly seminar for anyone interested in symbolic computation, run by the Istanbul node from 2021 to 2023. Meetings took place on Fridays at 17:00 at IMBM (Boğaziçi University, South Campus, Bebek, Istanbul) and were streamed on Zoom, so that people interested in symbolic computation could meet and exchange ideas. Tülay Ayyıldız served on the organising committee in 2021–2022.
