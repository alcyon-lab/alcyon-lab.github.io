Eugenia Maria Betsani, an undergraduate student in the Athens node, presents joint work with Zafeirakis Zafeirakopoulos at [CASC 2026](https://www.casc-conference.org/) in Bath, UK: *Sparse multivariate interpolation and rational generating functions of polyhedra*, on using sparse interpolation to efficiently compute rational generating functions of lattice points in polyhedra.
