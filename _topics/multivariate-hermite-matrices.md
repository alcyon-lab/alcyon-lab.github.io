Hermite matrices of a zero-dimensional polynomial system can be computed from approximate roots and then certified (Journal of Symbolic Computation, 2023). This allows exact information, such as the number of real solutions, to be recovered from numerical data.

## Goal

Turn the theory into a working implementation for multivariate systems, and study its behaviour experimentally on regular, singular and near-singular examples.
