Isolating the real solutions of polynomial systems with guarantees is a central problem in real algebraic geometry. Symbolic methods are exact but slow, while numerical methods are fast but uncertified.

## Goal

Develop certified symbolic-numeric algorithms for real solution isolation, building on Hermite matrices and rational univariate representations, with complexity analysis and implementation.
