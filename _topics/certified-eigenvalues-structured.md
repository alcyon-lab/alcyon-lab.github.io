Certified Real Eigenvalue Location (SYNASC 2025) combines symbolic and numeric computation to locate the real eigenvalues of a matrix with guarantees. Structured matrices offer both opportunities for speed-ups and new difficulties.

## Goal

Adapt the method to structured classes such as symmetric, tridiagonal and polynomial eigenvalue problems, implement it, and compare it with interval-based approaches.
