A polynomial that is a sum of squares is non-negative, and sums-of-squares decompositions can be found by semidefinite programming. The numerical output, however, is only approximate.

## Goal

Compute sums-of-squares decompositions numerically, round and project them to exact rational certificates, and study when this succeeds.
